# Conduct risk MI note: July 2026

Prepared 6 October 2026. Covers 7,857 published ombudsman decisions dated June 2026 to September 2026, plus FCA complaints returns to 2025H2.

The ombudsman publishes decisions several weeks after issuing them, so August (1,540 decisions, published to 18 August) and September (1 decision, published to 15 September) are incomplete. They count in the totals but not in month-on-month comparisons.

## Headlines

The ombudsman upheld 24% of complaints across the period.
The fastest-growing themes in July were Account closure, blocks and restrictions (95 decisions, +11 on the previous month); Credit file and data errors (34 decisions, +2 on the previous month).
18 firms are red on the watchlist and a further 35 amber (a firm counts once, at its worst signal).
The ombudsman-decision early-warning test did not run. It needs 6 complete months of history before the month tested, and this data has 1 before July 2026. The watchlist therefore rests on the FCA returns alone.

## FCA complaints returns

Firms in the FCA's firm-level data reported 1,652,438 complaints opened in 2025H2, 5.0% down on 2025H1 (1,740,222).
By product group: Banking and credit cards 844,807; Insurance and pure protection 620,885; Decumulation and pensions 80,272; Home finance 66,543; Investments 39,931.
Against their own history and their peers, 18 firms are red on at least one product group and a further 35 amber.

## Caveats

Published decisions are complaints that reached an ombudsman, not every complaint a firm receives. FCA returns cover firms reporting 500 or more complaints in six months, and firms report on their own financial year, so periods don't align exactly. Theme tags are rule-based and reviewed, not perfect.
